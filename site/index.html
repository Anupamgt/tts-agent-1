Questions

Who is Deskie for?

A clinic with a few doctors and one receptionist. Deskie covers the calls that arrive while someone is already with a patient.

Is the voice a person?

No. The opening line says it is the clinic’s automated assistant. A person joins only when the caller asks for the desk.

Where do the hours and the fee come from?

From the clinic’s records, not from the model’s memory. Staff can add a clinic, a doctor, a patient history note, and a desk user after they sign in, under Records.

What should I not try on this demo call?

Do not read out a real patient’s details. The clinic on this number is a demo, with fictional files already loaded.

What runs the call?

The agent runs on LiveKit Agents on LiveKit Cloud. Gnani Prisma hears English, Hindi and Hinglish, Gnani Timbre v2.5 speaks in the Hinglish voice Poorvi, and an OpenAI-compatible model with tool calling (gpt-4o-mini today) decides the reply and reads the clinic’s records. Gnani’s open-weight Evon v3.3 can be plugged in through vLLM.

How does a call reach Deskie?

Browser calls go through LiveKit over WebRTC, and any SIP trunk, such as Vobiz, plugs into LiveKit SIP. The desk number is also available on Bolna + Vobiz telephony.
