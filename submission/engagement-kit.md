Shortlisting is decided by **verified** reactions, comments and reshares on the nominated posts, counted at the closing cutoff (11:59 PM IST, 10 Nov 2026). The rules disqualify bought engagement, bots, paid promotion and engagement swaps. Everything below is organic: real people who would genuinely care.
