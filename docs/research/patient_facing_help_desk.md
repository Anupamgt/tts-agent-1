# Patient-Facing Hospital Help Desk (Front Desk / Enquiry Desk / Patient Help Desk / Patient Relations / Guest Relations)

Research scope note: Sources are hospital websites (AIIMS New Delhi, Mayo Clinic, Milton Keynes University Hospital NHS, Homerton NHS, Georgetown Public Hospital Guyana), India's National Health Systems Resource Centre (NHSRC) grievance help desk guidelines, Indian hospital job postings (2025-2026), a 2026 student poster from Salem Hospital (US), and Indian news reports from 2026. Searched 2026-10-18. I did not fetch any Beryl Institute or peer-reviewed patient-experience literature, so the literature side is thin (see Gaps).

## 1. Core functions of a hospital help desk / information desk

### Takeaway
Across hospitals, the help desk covers four groups of work. (1) Greeting, information and wayfinding. (2) Front-office transactions such as registration, UHID/OPD cards, appointments, and help with admission and discharge. (3) Taking and routing complaints and feedback. (4) Coordination: emergency prioritisation, sending patients on to other departments, and giving updates. Which of these a given desk does depends on whether it is a volunteer "welcome desk" (as at Mayo or NHS trusts), a staffed front office (as in Indian private hospitals), or a grievance help desk (as under India's public-health system).

### Cited Findings
**Greeting, information and wayfinding (volunteer/information-desk model)**
- Mayo Clinic (Minnesota) runs volunteer "Information and Wayfinding" roles in four types. Admissions and Desk C volunteers welcome patients, answer questions, give basic information and directions, and "provide friendly conversation to help patients feel less apprehensive." Elevator-assistance volunteers work in lobbies and subways and help people with physical challenges reach elevators. Greeters walk hallways and approach people who look lost. Welcome desks across the campuses give out information, room numbers and directions. — [Mayo Clinic Volunteers: Information and Wayfinding](https://mayoclinic.org/about-mayo-clinic/volunteers/minnesota/service-areas/information-wayfinding)
- At Milton Keynes University Hospital (NHS, UK), "Meet and Greet/Wayfinder" volunteers stand at entrances, crossroads and other busy areas. They greet people, direct patients and visitors to the right areas, and answer "basic queries" and non-medical questions. — [MKUH Meet and Greet/Wayfinders](https://www.mkuh.nhs.uk/?p=33725)
- AIIMS New Delhi has a Central Enquiry & Registration office "just inside main entrance on Aurobindo Marg" (phone 26588500/26588900/26588700, ext. 4707). It also has "enquiry counters and help desks… at various locations" in the OPD, and guide maps, display boards and LED scrolls "at various strategic locations." — [AIIMS New Delhi Citizen Charter: General Information](https://aiims.edu/aiims/hosp-serv/citizen-charter/general-inf.htm)
- Georgetown Public Hospital Corporation (Guyana) opened a 24/7 Help Desk, the "Orange Office", at Gate #1 (Main Gate) on 24 January 2024. The hospital calls it a "central hub for information and assistance." It gives directions and patient updates, helps people access services, and takes recommendations, commendations and complaints about service or staff. People can reach it by phone, a dedicated WhatsApp number, email or in person. — [Guyana Chronicle, 24 Jan 2024](https://guyanachronicle.com/2024/01/24/gphc-introduces-24-7-help-desk/)

**Front-office transactions (Indian private hospital model)**
- A Front Office & Patient Relations Executive posting at Prime Indian Hospital, Chennai (apply by 12 Nov 2026) lists these duties: welcoming patients, visitors and guests; registration, appointment scheduling and confirmations; managing front-desk operations and patient flow; answering questions about services, departments, doctors and appointments; helping with admission, discharge and departmental guidance; handling calls and emails; dealing with complaints and feedback and escalating when needed; managing visitor entries; guiding patients on billing and payment; supporting emergency coordination and priority handling; keeping reception and waiting areas presentable; and preparing daily reports on appointments, enquiries and front-office activity. — [Drlogy job listing: Front Office & Patient Relations, Prime Indian Hospital](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457)
- A Front Office Executive (OPD/IPD) posting in Mumbai, apparently RG Hospitals (the employer is not confirmed on the page), lists these duties: registering new patients and recording demographics, including "how they heard of the hospital"; preparing patient files and OPD cards and issuing UHID numbers; guiding patients through the next steps of their visit; coordinating with doctors and consultants; giving information about doctors and services; handling admissions and billing and covering reception; resolving visitor queries; prioritising emergency cases and coordinating with consultants; maintaining records; and taking part in QA/QC and continuous quality improvement. The role reports to the Manager, Out Patient. — [Talentmate job listing: Front Office Executive OPD/IPD, Mumbai](https://www.talentmate.com/jobs/india/mumbai/front-office-executive-opd-ipd/2607-6809-48)

**Grievance / feedback function (Indian public-sector model)**
- Under NHSRC's guidelines for a Grievance Redressal (GR) Help Desk in public facilities, the desk receives complaints orally or in writing (including from the suggestion box and referrals from the call centre or web portal). It registers every grievance on a web portal, refers denials of service or entitlement to the nodal officer for immediate help, and aims to resolve "60-70% of grievances immediately at the local level." It also runs random exit interviews of about 2 OPD/IPD clients per day, flags infrastructure and amenity gaps to the Medical Officer In-Charge, and displays patient rights next to the desk. — [NHSRC, Guidelines for Establishing Grievance Redressal Help Desks](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)

**Admission/discharge escort and non-clinical patient support (ward-level patient relations)**
- A "Patient Relation Officer" posting at GEM Hospital, Chennai, which the description also calls "Patient Care Executive", goes well beyond a desk. It covers guiding patients and attendants to wards and rooms; coordinating with the admission desk, nursing station and billing; explaining discharge steps, making sure belongings are returned and helping arrange transport; passing on requests and complaints; coordinating with dietary, housekeeping and maintenance; collecting feedback and sending it to the quality team; and helping resolve grievances. It also lists hands-on duties such as helping with mobility, hygiene and feeding, and carrying samples and documents. — [Recooty job listing: GEM Hospital Patient Relation Officer](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105)

**Social welfare and control room (AIIMS)**
- AIIMS New Delhi has a Medical Social Services office at Room No. 9, Ground Floor, OPD, where MSSOs and Social Guides are available "for any help." Its Control Room at Room No. 12, Ground Floor, Private Ward Block (M.S. Office), "functions round the clock" with Duty Officers from the Dept. of Hospital Administration, who "listen to public grievances with regard to patient care." — [AIIMS New Delhi Citizen Charter](https://aiims.edu/aiims/hosp-serv/citizen-charter/general-inf.htm)

**Display of doctor/OPD information (policy context, Kerala)**
- On 24 Sep 2026, the Kerala Health Department ordered all government health institutions, from PHCs to taluk headquarters hospitals, to display sanctioned, working and vacant doctor posts, each doctor's OP timings (updated regularly), grievance redressal details, and the phone numbers of the Deputy DMO (Vigilance) and the hospital superintendent. The order followed complaints that doctors were unavailable during OP hours. The article does not mention a help desk. — [Deccan Chronicle/PTI, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/southern-states/kerala/keralam-govt-directs-state-run-hospitals-to-display-doctors-details-op-timings-1990149)

### Inferences
- The checklist in the brief (wayfinding, registration and appointments, doctor/OPD timings, visitor management, complaints, billing queries, admission and discharge help, emergency routing) is covered by the cited Indian private-hospital job descriptions (Prime Indian Hospital, Mumbai OPD/IPD). In mid-sized Indian private hospitals, one "Front Office/Patient Relations Executive" role often does all of these.
- Wheelchair/escort and lost-and-found appear only indirectly: Mayo's elevator assistance, and the GEM role's patient transfers and return of belongings. No source fetched treats them as named help-desk functions.

### Gaps
- No source fetched names lost-and-found as a help-desk function. The AIIMS citizen charter page does not mention wheelchair/trolley services or lost and found.
- I did not find published, verified descriptions from large Indian private chains (Apollo, Fortis, Max, Manipal, Narayana) of their help-desk or guest-relations function. The Apollo BGS and Narayana Health postings I found had sparse or removed content.

## 2. How the help desk differs from and overlaps with reception/registration, admissions, billing, patient relations/PRO, and the call centre

### Takeaway
The lines between these desks are blurry, and they depend on hospital size and sector. Large public hospitals split them physically: AIIMS has separate enquiry/registration, social services and a grievance control room. NHS trusts split information/wayfinding volunteers from PALS. India's public system adds a separate grievance help desk linked to a call centre and web portal. Smaller private hospitals merge front office, patient relations and some billing and admission duties into one role.

### Cited Findings
- **Merged model (Indian private):** A single Front Office & Patient Relations Executive handles registration, appointments, admission and discharge guidance, billing and payment guidance, phone and email, complaints with escalation, and visitor entries. — [Prime Indian Hospital listing](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457). A Mumbai Front Office Executive (OPD/IPD) is asked to "handle admissions and billing, and cover reception duties when needed." — [Talentmate listing](https://www.talentmate.com/jobs/india/mumbai/front-office-executive-opd-ipd/2607-6809-48)
- **Patient relations vs. admission desk vs. billing:** The GEM Hospital Patient Relation Officer is told to "coordinate with the admission desk, nursing station, and billing." That makes these distinct units, with patient relations acting as the liaison and escort between them. — [GEM Hospital listing](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105)
- **Physically separated model (AIIMS):** At AIIMS New Delhi, the Central Enquiry & Registration office sits at the main entrance, enquiry counters and help desks are spread across the OPD, the Medical Social Services office is in Room 9 of the OPD, and grievances go to a 24-hour Control Room run by Duty Officers of Hospital Administration. — [AIIMS Citizen Charter](https://aiims.edu/aiims/hosp-serv/citizen-charter/general-inf.htm)
- **Help desk vs. call centre vs. web portal (Indian public grievance system):** NHSRC sets up the help desk as "one of three channels (with the call centre and web portal) feeding a single web portal." The call centre can refer complaints to the desk, and every grievance is registered on the portal. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- **Information desk vs. PALS (UK):** At Homerton (NHS), PALS is a separate service with a drop-in at the main entrance, plus email, phone, textphone, post and an online form. It describes itself as a confidential point of contact for concerns about care that also takes compliments. Patients are asked to "seek resolution through PALS first," and PALS advises on the formal complaints process if they are still unhappy. — [Homerton Healthcare PALS](https://www.homerton.nhs.uk/patient-advice-liaison-service-pals). In the same NHS system, volunteer wayfinders answer only "basic queries" and non-medical questions. — [MKUH](https://www.mkuh.nhs.uk/?p=33725)
- **Combined help desk + feedback (Guyana):** GPHC's 24/7 Orange Office gives directions and updates and also takes complaints and commendations, which merges information and patient relations at one point. — [Guyana Chronicle](https://guyanachronicle.com/2024/01/24/gphc-introduces-24-7-help-desk/)

### Inferences
- A workable split: **information/help desk**, for directions, general queries and first contact; **registration/front office**, for transactions such as UHID, OPD card and appointments; **admissions desk**, for IPD admission paperwork and bed allotment; **billing desk**, for estimates, deposits, final bills and insurance; **patient relations/PRO/PALS**, for complaints, feedback, escalation and in-patient liaison; **call centre**, for remote appointments and enquiries. The help desk is usually the routing layer that sends people to the others.
- In NHS practice, the formal complaints office sits a step above PALS. In Indian public hospitals, the nodal officer sits above the help desk, with district and state escalation beyond that.

### Gaps
- I found no source that explicitly sets out a model for where an insurance/TPA desk sits relative to the help desk. Billing and insurance guidance appears only as one duty in front-office job descriptions.
- I found no source on how Indian private hospital call centres hand off to on-site help desks.

## 3. A typical shift and the most common tasks by volume

### Takeaway
I found very little hard data on enquiry volumes. The only empirical source, a 2026 student poster at Salem Hospital (US), found most questions were about directions, finding departments and clarifying procedures, with demand peaking at the main entrance, waiting areas and elevators. Shift structure is best documented in the NHSRC guidelines: 24/7 operator cover in three 8-hour shifts, plus a day-shift coordinator.

### Cited Findings
- Salem Hospital (US) logged visitors' frequently asked questions and tracked high-traffic locations. Most questions were about directions, finding departments and clarifying hospital procedures. Demand peaked at the main entrance, waiting areas and near elevators, "especially at the busiest times of day." Limited directional signage added to confusion. No counts were published in the abstract. — [Nkulu, "A Helping Hand In Healthcare," Salem State Univ. poster, Apr 2026](https://digitalrepository.salemstate.edu/handle/20.500.13013/3923)
- The NHSRC shift table has one coordinator on 10:00-18:00 and operators across 07:00-15:00, 15:00-23:00 and 23:00-07:00, three operators in total. The guideline text also says "three operators in each shift," which contradicts the table. Shift personnel names must be updated on the portal after every shift change. Recommended staffing is 4-12 at medical colleges, 4 at district hospitals and 1 at other eligible institutions, with no more than 4 desks per facility. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- NHSRC performance indicators include grievances registered and resolved per month, same-day resolutions, and OPD/IPD exit forms filled. A monthly report goes to the Rogi Kalyan Samiti (RKS), the District Collector and the CMO. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- Private-hospital front-office roles must "prepare daily reports on appointments, inquiries, and front office activity." This points to daily logging of enquiry volume, though no figures are published. — [Prime Indian Hospital listing](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457)
- The GEM Patient Relation Officer role includes "attend daily briefings" and updating "status logs, shift handover notes, and movement registers." — [GEM Hospital listing](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105)
- Mumbai Front Office Executive duty hours are "as per scheduled requirements," with a set dress code and ID card. — [Talentmate listing](https://www.talentmate.com/jobs/india/mumbai/front-office-executive-opd-ipd/2607-6809-48)

### Inferences
- Combining the Salem poster with the OPD-centred Indian job descriptions, the highest-volume tasks are likely: (1) directions and department location; (2) doctor availability, OPD timings and appointment questions; (3) registration, UHID and next-step guidance; (4) admission, discharge and billing process questions; (5) complaints, which are lower in volume but take more time. This ranking is inferred, not measured.
- A front-office shift probably has a morning OPD rush, which is when registration and wayfinding load is highest. Shift-handover registers and daily reports are standard. The OPD peak is not measured in the sources found.

### Gaps
- I found no published quantitative breakdown of help-desk enquiry types or volumes from an Indian hospital, and none of a hospital-wide breakdown from anywhere.
- I found no published "day in the life" or minute-by-minute shift description.

## 4. Role in grievance/complaint handling, patient feedback and patient experience

### Takeaway
The help desk is usually the first-line, informal resolution point. It takes complaints, fixes what it can on the spot, logs everything and escalates the rest on set timelines. It also often collects feedback through exit interviews, feedback forms and passing comments to the quality team. India's NHSRC model is the most fully specified example: 60-70% of grievances resolved locally, a 7-day facility deadline, and escalation to district at day 14, to state after day 15, and to the Health Minister or Chief Minister at day 21.

### Cited Findings
- NHSRC timelines and escalation: urgent issues such as denial of services or entitlements get immediate or real-time resolution, and facility-level grievances must be resolved within 7 days. The nodal officer gets an SMS reminder 2 days before the deadline. After 7 days a case escalates to district level, with resolution expected by day 14. It then goes to the State Nodal Officer, with the Principal Secretary or Mission Director involved after 15 days. Cases still unresolved at 21 days go to the Health Minister and/or Chief Minister. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- NHSRC record formats: the Annexure 3 registration format captures a reference number, complainant details, grievance category and description, relief sought, and whether the grievance was lodged before. The Annexure 5 monitoring tool checks suggestion-box availability and how often it is opened, feedback forms held and filled, the complaint database, and how approachable desk staff are and how they liaise with others. The desk works under the facility's Rogi Kalyan Samiti, which reviews grievances and approves corrective actions that cost money. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- NHSRC tone guidance: the desk should act "as a facilitator rather than an intimidating outside party," keep complainants' identities confidential and respond with empathy. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- PALS (UK) is the informal first step before formal complaints. It resolves concerns, gives confidential advice, takes compliments and points people to advocacy services. — [Homerton PALS](https://www.homerton.nhs.uk/patient-advice-liaison-service-pals)
- In Indian private hospitals, the front office "address[es] complaints and feedback, and escalate[s] when necessary" and runs "patient relations activities to improve satisfaction and service quality." — [Prime Indian Hospital listing](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457). Patient Relation Officers "collect patient feedback and forward it to the quality team" and "contribute to satisfaction scores." — [GEM Hospital listing](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105). Senior guest relations staff "resolve guest complaints," "monitor guest feedback," "develop guest service standards" and "train staff on guest relations." — [Apollo BGS Hospitals Senior GRE listing (via Semesteria)](https://app.semesteria.com/jobs/senior-guest-relations-executive-chennai-apollo-bgs-hospitals-415bf491-4bda-4268-9cee-3c56d6d9fe15)
- Kerala context (31 Jul 2026): after complaints of "inadequate assistance, rude behaviour, poor communication," the Kerala government ordered patient-friendly behaviour training for all non-Medical-Officer staff in government hospitals, including security and contract staff. Topics include communication, public relations, behavioural management and grievance redressal. One trigger was a July 2026 death at Neyyattinkara General Hospital, where the family said security staff made a man with chest pain wait. Staff must finish the training within two months of the order. — [Onmanorama, 31 Jul 2026](https://www.onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2026/07/31/amid-complaints-kerala-to-train-hospital-staff-on-patient-friendly-behaviour.html)

### Inferences
- The Kerala episode shows why first-contact staff (gate, security, enquiry) matter for emergency routing: in the Neyyattinkara case, the family alleged that a delay at first contact came before the death. This is an allegation reported in the press, not a finding. Emergency prioritisation shows up explicitly in the Indian front-office job descriptions.
- NHSRC's 60-70% local-resolution target suggests most grievances are operational (waiting, availability, entitlements, amenities) and can be fixed by liaising on the spot.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch or verify NABH accreditation standards or NABH's complaint-handling policy documents (search surfaced NABH "Policy & Procedure for Handling Complaints," Issues 5 and 7, and the "Quality Setu" platform). NABH's specific requirements for internal patient grievance handling are therefore not documented here.
- I found no data on how many complaints are first received at help desks versus other channels.
- I did not access the Beryl Institute or other patient-experience literature.

## 5. Typical job titles, qualifications and skills

### Takeaway
Indian titles include Front Office Executive, Front Desk Executive, Front Office & Patient Relations Executive, Guest Relations Executive (GRE)/Senior GRE, Patient Relation(s) Officer/Associate, Patient Care Executive and Customer Relations Coordinator. The typical entry requirement is any graduate degree with 0-3 years' experience. Senior guest relations roles ask for 5+ years. Core skills are communication, customer service, HIS/computer use, confidentiality, coordination and conflict resolution. In the West, much of the wayfinding and greeting work is done by volunteers.

### Cited Findings
- Front Office Executive (OPD/IPD), Mumbai: graduate in any stream, 1-3 years' experience, dress code and ID card, reports to the Manager, Out Patient. — [Talentmate](https://www.talentmate.com/jobs/india/mumbai/front-office-executive-opd-ipd/2607-6809-48)
- Front Office & Patient Relations Executive, Prime Indian Hospital, Chennai: graduate, 1 year's experience, open to all genders and any language, full-time and on-site. — [Drlogy](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457)
- Senior Guest Relations Executive, Apollo BGS Hospitals (listed as Chennai): bachelor's degree, 5+ years in guest relations. Must-have skills are leadership, communication, problem-solving, customer service and teamwork. Nice-to-have skills are healthcare knowledge, conflict resolution, training, analytical thinking and multilingual ability. — [Semesteria listing](https://app.semesteria.com/jobs/senior-guest-relations-executive-chennai-apollo-bgs-hospitals-415bf491-4bda-4268-9cee-3c56d6d9fe15)
- Patient Relation Officer / Patient Care Executive, GEM Hospital, Chennai: tagged mid-senior level. The role mixes liaison work with hands-on patient-care support and requires infection-control training. No qualification is listed. — [Recooty](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105)
- NHSRC grievance help desk: the coordinator must be at least a graduate with at least 2 years' experience, and operators may be fresh graduates. Both need computer skills, knowledge of health systems, clinical services and health programmes, and good communication. Staff are employed by an outsourced agency on a 3-year MoU. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- Other Indian titles found in search results but not fetched or verified: "Guest Relation Executive" (NHPL, ASG Eye Hospital, SP Fort Hospital, though the SP Fort listing returned "Job Not Found"), "Patient Relations Associate" (Docthub), "Customer Relations Coordinator – Patient Care Services" (Drlogy, Bangalore) and "Front Desk Executive" (Dr Ram's Advanced Surgical Hospital, Ahmedabad). — [Search results; e.g., Drlogy Customer Relations Coordinator](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/bangalore/customer-relations-coordinator-patient-care-services-1510)
- International volunteer model: Mayo Clinic and MKUH (NHS) fill greeting, welcome-desk and wayfinder roles with volunteers. MKUH asks for "strong communication skills, an approachable manner, and a willingness to help." — [MKUH](https://www.mkuh.nhs.uk/?p=33725); [Mayo Clinic](https://mayoclinic.org/about-mayo-clinic/volunteers/minnesota/service-areas/information-wayfinding)
- AIIMS uses MSSOs and "Social Guides" for patient help, and Duty Officers from Hospital Administration for grievances. — [AIIMS Citizen Charter](https://aiims.edu/aiims/hosp-serv/citizen-charter/general-inf.htm)

### Inferences
- In India, the help desk is a paid, entry-level, graduate role that commonly grows into Senior GRE or Patient Relations Manager roles. Hospital-administration degrees (MHA/BHA) are not required in the postings found.
- Western hospitals tend to split the work into volunteer wayfinding and professional PALS or patient-relations staff. Indian private hospitals tend to combine it into paid GRE and front-office roles.

### Gaps
- I found no verified salary ranges. The Prime Indian Hospital listing says "as per experience," and the Docthub career page was blocked by robots.txt.
- I found no data on the share of help-desk staff holding specific certifications, such as NABH front-office training or hospitality diplomas.

## 6. Departments the help desk coordinates with, and how escalations work

### Takeaway
The help desk coordinates with doctors and consultants, nursing stations, admissions, billing, emergency, diagnostics, dietary, housekeeping, maintenance, security, the quality team, medical social services and hospital administration. Escalation typically goes from front-line staff to a supervisor or Manager (Out Patient) or a nodal officer, then to hospital administration and the medical superintendent. In Indian public facilities it continues to district and state authorities on fixed timelines.

### Cited Findings
- Coordination partners in Indian private-hospital front-office roles: "doctors, nursing, billing, and other departments," plus emergency patient coordination. — [Prime Indian Hospital](https://www.drlogy.com/jobs/chennai/front-office-patient-relations-1457); "doctors and consultants," with emergency cases sent on to "relevant consultants or departments." — [Talentmate Mumbai](https://www.talentmate.com/jobs/india/mumbai/front-office-executive-opd-ipd/2607-6809-48)
- Patient Relation Officer coordination: admission desk, nursing station, billing, dietary, housekeeping, maintenance and the quality team. Unsafe conditions or incidents go "to supervisors immediately." — [GEM Hospital](https://domains.recooty.com/gem-hospital/patient-relation-officer-ge105)
- Senior GRE: "collaborate with healthcare teams." — [Apollo BGS listing](https://app.semesteria.com/jobs/senior-guest-relations-executive-chennai-apollo-bgs-hospitals-415bf491-4bda-4268-9cee-3c56d6d9fe15)
- NHSRC escalation chain: desk operator, then desk coordinator, then the facility nodal officer (a medical officer, hospital manager or other staff, who resolves the grievance and closes it on the portal), then the MOI/C or head of institution and the RKS, then district (by day 14), then state (after day 15), then the Health Minister and/or Chief Minister (day 21). The coordinator liaises with the nodal officer, facility staff and complainants "especially for urgent cases," and desk staff may attend internal hospital meetings and RKS meetings. — [NHSRC Guidelines](https://www.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-05/Grievance%20Redressal.pdf)
- AIIMS route: enquiry desk, then the Control Room (Duty Officers, Hospital Administration, M.S. Office, 24×7). Medical Social Services handles welfare needs. — [AIIMS Citizen Charter](https://aiims.edu/aiims/hosp-serv/citizen-charter/general-inf.htm)
- Kerala's 2026 order requires hospitals to display the phone numbers of the Deputy DMO (Vigilance) and the hospital superintendent, which creates a public escalation route that bypasses the front desk. — [Deccan Chronicle/PTI](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/southern-states/kerala/keralam-govt-directs-state-run-hospitals-to-display-doctors-details-op-timings-1990149)
- NHS: PALS sends unresolved concerns to the formal Complaints Service. — [Homerton PALS](https://www.homerton.nhs.uk/patient-advice-liaison-service-pals)

### Inferences
- In a private Indian hospital, the escalation chain is likely front office/GRE, then Front Office or Patient Relations Manager (or Manager, Out Patient), then the Medical Superintendent or Unit Head and the Quality department. This is pieced together from the reporting lines and "escalate when necessary" clauses in job descriptions. No full SOP was found.
- Help desks also feed the Quality department through feedback, satisfaction scores and QA/QI participation, which links them to accreditation work such as NABH.

### Gaps
- I found no published SOP from an Indian private hospital that sets out escalation levels and turnaround times for complaints received at the front desk.
- I did not research TAT benchmarks beyond NHSRC (7/14/21 days).
